The Honest Truth

We weren't meant to
raise families in isolation.

Modern parents are drowning in child-rearing information on social media and parenting blogs — yet starving for practical, face-to-face support. The playbooks we were handed don't match the lives we're actually living.

The Geographic Shift

Grandparents live hours away. Multigenerational support has become a luxury few families can access. The biological family may no longer be the healthy network a modern parent needs.

The Digital Paradox

One thousand parenting experts on Instagram. Zero hands to hold a crying baby. More connected than any generation before us. More alone than any generation before us.

The Chosen Clan

The biological family may not supply the reciprocity and values you need. We have the opportunity — and the responsibility — to intentionally build our chosen village.

Founder & Builder
Founder Story

I was always the one
who showed up.

My entire life, I've been the person people called when they needed help. As the oldest of five children, caring for others wasn't something I learned — it was something I lived.

That became my career. For more than fifteen years, I worked as a nanny, family assistant, caregiver, chef, and childcare professional. I had a front-row seat to the realities of family life.

I saw exhausted parents doing everything alone. Mothers carrying invisible mental loads. Families searching for childcare, educational support, community, and guidance — and coming up empty.

For years, I thought these were isolated problems. Eventually, I realized they were all symptoms of the same thing.

The village is disappearing.

But there was a harder question underneath everything I was witnessing: Who is the village for the village?

That question changed everything. I began to understand that what so many people are searching for isn't perfection, or more information, or another app. They're searching for connection. For belonging. For people who check in. For people who show up. For people who choose them.

What became clear was that the village many of us need isn't always the one we were born into. Sometimes we have to build it. Sometimes the strongest communities are formed through shared values rather than shared DNA.

And then there were the people who showed up for me — in ways I will never have enough words for. They reminded me that the village isn't always where you expect to find it. Sometimes it finds you.

That's why I founded Thrivehaus. Because every family needs a village. We're building yours.

— Jennifer Aguilar

Founder, Thrivehaus
